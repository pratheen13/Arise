ARISE 4.2 — Samsung Health source option

Profile > Samsung Health only is enabled by default for this update. Steps and distance use the Samsung Health source filter, excluding separate Android-phone and other app sources. Turning it off returns to Health Connect combined totals, using its priority and deduplication rules.

Samsung Health shares its All steps value. That can include phone and watch activity. ARISE cannot guarantee a watch-only total through this source filter. The update does not establish that the previous combined total was double-counted: device totals were not available to compare.

On the phone, open Samsung Health > Settings > Health Connect and allow Steps to be written/shared. Connect the watch through Galaxy Wearable, then open Samsung Health and pull down to sync. Open ARISE > Activity > Refresh activity. If Samsung has not shared today's steps, the app explains this rather than silently using another source.

The first refresh replaces old mixed-source activity totals; XP and completed quests are preserved. Estimated distance remains labeled and is based on the selected source's steps.

Samsung documentation: https://developer.samsung.com/health/blog/en/accessing-samsung-health-data-through-health-connect
